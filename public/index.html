Hi, I'm Divas. I have a MacBook and an Android phone, and I wanted iPhone Mirroring for my phone. Apple wasn't going to build that, so I made Phone Mirror.

It is a friendly menu on top of scrcpy, the open-source tool that does the heavy lifting. There is no account, and it never goes online.

Questions

Do I have to install anything on my phone?

No. For mirroring you turn on one setting, USB debugging, and tap Allow the first time you plug the phone in. For sending files with Quick Share you change nothing on the phone at all. Nothing stays installed on it.

How does Quick Share to the Mac work?

Quick Share is the sharing that is already built into Android. Tick Receive with Quick Share in Phone Mirror's menu and your Mac shows up in the phone's Quick Share list while both are on the same Wi-Fi. Every share waits for you to click Accept on the Mac. It goes one way, from the phone to the Mac; to put files on the phone, use Phone Files. The setup guide has the steps.

Which phones work?

Android phones with Android 5 or newer; sound needs Android 11 or newer. I built and tested it with a POCO X4 Pro on Android 13. Xiaomi, POCO and Redmi phones need one extra switch, and the setup guide shows where it is.

Does it work without a cable?

Yes. Plug the cable in once, click Go Wireless, then unplug. Phone and Mac have to be on the same Wi-Fi, or the Mac can join the phone's hotspot. After the phone restarts you plug in once more.

Will there be lag?

With a cable it feels close to using the phone. Over Wi-Fi it depends on your network: a 5 GHz network or Hotspot Mode is smooth, a busy 2.4 GHz router can stutter. The menu shows you the frame rate and the connection quality.

Why does my Mac warn me when I open it?

Apple checks apps for developers who pay for its yearly programme, and I haven't. So macOS asks you to confirm before it runs. The download page shows the four steps, and the security page explains how to check the download yourself.

Is it safe to turn on USB debugging?

USB debugging lets a computer you have approved control the phone, which is how Phone Mirror works. Only tap Allow for your own Mac. The security page has the details and how to switch it off again.

iPhone, Intel Mac or Windows?

No. Phone Mirror is for Android phones and Macs with Apple Silicon. iPhones have Apple's own iPhone Mirroring.

Something isn't working. Who do I ask?

Write to contact@phonemirror.in. Say which phone you have and what the first line of the menu says. The quick fixes cover the usual problems.
