Hi, I'm Divas. I have a MacBook and an Android phone, and I wanted iPhone Mirroring for my phone. Apple wasn't going to build that, so I made Phone Mirror.

It is a friendly menu on top of scrcpy, the open-source tool that does the heavy lifting. There is no account. The only thing it sends anywhere is an anonymous count once a day, so I know how many people use it, and you can switch that off.

Questions

What is Phone Mirror?

Phone Mirror is a Mac app for Android screen mirroring. It sits in the menu bar and shows your Android phone's screen in a window on your Mac, over a USB cable or Wi-Fi. You control the phone with the Mac's keyboard and pointer, drag files both ways, and receive files with Quick Share.

How do I mirror my Android phone's screen on my Mac?

Download Phone Mirror and drag it into Applications. On the phone, turn on USB debugging, once. Plug the phone into the Mac, tap Allow on the phone, and click Mirror Phone in the menu bar. After that first time you can go wireless. The setup guide has the steps for your phone's brand.

Do I have to install anything on my phone?

No. For mirroring you turn on one setting, USB debugging, and tap Allow the first time you plug the phone in. For sending files with Quick Share you change nothing on the phone at all. Nothing stays installed on it.

How does Quick Share to the Mac work?

Quick Share is the sharing that is already built into Android. Tick Receive with Quick Share in Phone Mirror's menu and your Mac shows up in the phone's Quick Share list while both are on the same Wi-Fi. Every share waits for you to click Accept on the Mac. It goes one way, from the phone to the Mac; to put files on the phone, use Phone Files. The setup guide has the steps.

Can I send files from Android to a Mac like AirDrop?

Yes. Phone Mirror doesn't use AirDrop, but it works like AirDrop: tick Receive with Quick Share and your Mac shows up in the phone's Quick Share list. There is no cable and nothing to install on the phone. Both have to be on the same Wi-Fi. Click Accept on the Mac and the file is in your Downloads folder.

Which phones work?

Android phones with Android 5 or newer; sound needs Android 11 or newer. I built and tested it with a POCO X4 Pro on Android 13. Xiaomi, POCO and Redmi phones need one extra switch, and the setup guide shows where it is.

Does it work without a cable?

Yes. Plug the cable in once, click Go Wireless, then unplug. Phone and Mac have to be on the same Wi-Fi, or the Mac can join the phone's hotspot. After the phone restarts you plug in once more.

Will there be lag?

With a cable it feels close to using the phone. Over Wi-Fi it depends on your network: a 5 GHz network or Hotspot Mode is smooth, a busy 2.4 GHz router can stutter. The menu shows you the frame rate and the connection quality.

Why does my Mac warn me when I open it?

Apple checks apps for developers who pay for its yearly programme, and I haven't. So macOS asks you to confirm before it runs. The download page shows the four steps, and the security page explains how to check the download yourself.

Is it safe to turn on USB debugging?

USB debugging lets a computer you have approved control the phone, which is how Phone Mirror works. Only tap Allow for your own Mac. The security page has the details and how to switch it off again.

Is there iPhone Mirroring for Android?

Apple's iPhone Mirroring only works with an iPhone. Phone Mirror is what I built to get the same idea with an Android phone: its screen in a window on the Mac, the Mac's keyboard and pointer to control it, and the phone left in your pocket.

iPhone, Intel Mac or Windows?

No. Phone Mirror is for Android phones and Macs with Apple Silicon. iPhones have Apple's own iPhone Mirroring.

Why is a screen black in the mirror?

Android hides some screens from every kind of screen capture: app locks, password fields and payment screens in banking apps. Phone Mirror gets a black picture for those and cannot change that. The mouse and keyboard still reach the phone, so you can often type the PIN on the Mac without seeing it; otherwise do that one step on the phone.

Why does Return start a new line instead of sending my message?

The phone treats the Mac's Return key like the Return key of any keyboard, and most chat apps then start a new line. Many have a setting for it. In WhatsApp it is Settings → Chats → “Enter is send”; in Telegram it is Settings → Chat Settings → “Send with Enter”.

Why does the picture go soft for a moment when I swipe?

The phone squeezes its screen into video before sending it. When everything moves at once, as in a swipe between home screens, it has to drop detail for an instant, and the picture sharpens again as soon as it settles.

How do I get a newer version?

Phone Mirror does not update itself. Choose Check for Updates… in its menu, or open the download page: if the version there is higher than the one at the bottom of your menu, download it and drag it into Applications, choosing Replace. Your settings stay.

Something isn't working. Who do I ask?

Write to contact@phonemirror.in. Say which phone you have and what the first line of the menu says. The quick fixes cover the usual problems.
